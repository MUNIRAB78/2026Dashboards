Muslim Voter Outreach Impact: Primary → Runoff

Between the Democratic primary and the Plummer vs. Parker runoff, a targeted phone bank and mailer program aimed to increase turnout and support for Letitia Plummer in 241 precincts identified as having concentrations of registered Muslim voters. This dashboard measures whether that outreach shifted Plummer's vote share, precinct by precinct. Gain/loss is based on the change in Plummer's two-way vote share (Plummer ÷ [Plummer + Parker]) from the primary to the runoff, so it reflects persuasion within these precincts rather than simple turnout swings between the two elections.
